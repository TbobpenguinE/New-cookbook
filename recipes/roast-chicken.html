Herby Roast Chicken

This roast chicken is juicy on the inside with crispy skin on the outside.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken
  • 1 lemon, cut in half
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450 °F (230 °C).
  2. Rub chicken with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Roast for about 1 hour, until golden brown.
