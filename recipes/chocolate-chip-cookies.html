Chocolate Chip Cookies

These cookies are soft in the middle, crisp at the edges, and full of chocolate chips!

Ingredients

  • 2 ¼ cups flour
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • ¾ cup white sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 2 cups chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 °F (177 °C).
  2. Cream butter and sugars together.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla, then mix in dry ingredients.
  4. Stir in chocolate chips and bake 10–12 min.
